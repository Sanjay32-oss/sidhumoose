Biography

The man who revolutionized Punjabi music

Shubhdeep Singh Sidhu

Sidhu Moose Wala was born on June 11, 1993, in the village of Moosa, Mansa District, in Punjab, India. He emerged as one of the most influential Punjabi rappers and singers of his generation, bringing global recognition to Punjabi music.

After graduating from Guru Nanak Dev Engineering College with a degree in Electrical Engineering, Sidhu pursued his passion for music. He initially worked as a lyricist for established artists before launching his solo career, which skyrocketed to success with hits like "So High" and "Legend."

Named among The Guardian's 50 most promising artists in 2020, Sidhu became the first Punjabi artist to perform at Wireless Festival in London. His music addressed social issues, gangster lifestyle, and the struggles of youth in Punjab, resonating with millions globally.

Key Achievement: His songs charted on international platforms including UK and Canadian charts, breaking barriers for Punjabi music on the global stage.

Career Timeline

A journey from lyricist to global icon

2016 - The Beginning

Began career as a lyricist, writing for Ninja's hit "License." This breakthrough opened doors to the music industry and established his talent for meaningful lyrics.

2017 - Solo Breakthrough

Debuted with "G Wagon" followed by the massive hit "So High," which changed his career trajectory. Won Best Lyricist Award at Brit Asia TV Music Awards.

2018 - Album Release & Acting

Released debut album "PBX 1," which charted at #66 on Billboard Canadian Albums. Simultaneously launched acting career in Punjabi films like "Dakuaan Da Munda."

2019-2020 - Global Recognition

Singles "47" and "Mera Na" charted on UK Singles Chart. Named by The Guardian among 50 upcoming artists. Multiple Brit Asia TV Awards including Track of the Year.

2020 - Label Launch

Founded "5911 Records," his own record label. Released album "Snitches Get Stitches." Continued collaborations with international artists, expanding his global reach.

2021-2022 - Peak Success

Released albums "Moosetape" and "No Name." First Punjabi artist to perform at Wireless Festival London. Songs dominated charts across UK, Canada, and New Zealand markets.

Legacy & Impact

A legend whose influence continues to inspire

Sidhu Moose Wala was tragically assassinated on May 29, 2022, in his hometown of Jawaharke, Mansa District, at just 28 years old. His death sent shockwaves through the music world and deeply impacted his millions of fans globally.

Despite his brief life, Sidhu left an indelible mark on Punjabi music and global music culture. He transformed the perception of Punjabi music internationally and proved that regional artists could achieve mainstream global success.
